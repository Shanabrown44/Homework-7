Dances With Wolves

Dances with Wolves is one of my favorite films. Its the story of a Union soldier named John Dunbar, who is sent to a remote military post and develops a friendship with the Lakota people. He begins to see life differently as he learns about thier culture and way of life. He eventually marries a woman from the tribe named Stands with a fist and learns their ways. At first, they are not accepting of him, but slowly they begin to accept him as one of their own.
